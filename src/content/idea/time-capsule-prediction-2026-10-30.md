**Trick Name: "Time Capsule Prediction"**

**Effect:** The performer predicts a random word or phrase that a spectator will write on a piece of paper, which is then sealed in an envelope and kept secret until the reveal.

**Materials:**

* 5-6 envelopes (plain white or any other color)
* 5-6 pieces of paper (same size as the envelopes)
* A pen or pencil
* A small notebook or diary (optional)
* A random word or phrase list (prepared in advance)

**Preparation:**

1. Prepare a list of 5-6 random words or phrases (e.g., "summer vacation," "favorite book," " childhood memory," etc.). Write each word or phrase on a separate piece of paper, fold them, and place them in the corresponding envelopes. Seal the envelopes.
2. Keep the list of words or phrases secret.

**Performance:**

1. Ask a spectator to choose an envelope from the set. Make sure they don't see the contents of the other envelopes.
2. Once they've chosen an envelope, ask them to write a word or phrase on a piece of paper that comes to their mind
