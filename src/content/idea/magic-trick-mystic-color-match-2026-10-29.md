**Magic Trick: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Effect:** The magician predicts a randomly chosen color from a set of colored cards, and the prediction is revealed to be correct.

**Materials:**

* A set of 6-8 colored cards (e.g., red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, pink, and black)
* A small notebook or pad of paper
* A pen or pencil
* A sealed envelope (optional)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, write down a prediction on a piece of paper, such as "The chosen color will be blue." Fold the paper and place it in the sealed envelope, if using.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the set of colored cards and explain that you will try to predict the color they will choose.
3. **Color Selection:** Shuffle the colored cards and ask a spectator to choose one at random. They can either pick a card from the deck or you can show them the cards one by one and ask them to stop you at a random point.
