**Magic Trick: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Effect:** The magician predicts a randomly chosen color from a set of colored cards, and then reveals a matching colored sticker on a sealed envelope.

**Materials:**

* 6-8 colored cards (red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, and 1-2 additional colors of your choice)
* 6-8 colored stickers (matching the colors of the cards)
* 1 sealed envelope with a colored sticker on the inside (hidden from view)
* 1 prediction card or paper with a written message (e.g., "The chosen color is...")
* A randomization method (e.g., a dice, a random number generator, or a shuffled deck of cards)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, prepare the sealed envelope by placing a colored sticker on the inside. Write a prediction message on the prediction card, leaving a blank space for the color (e.g., "The chosen color is _______").
