**Magic Trick: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Effect:** The magician predicts a color chosen by a spectator from a set of colored cards, and the prediction is revealed to be correct.

**Materials:**

* 5 colored cards (red, blue, green, yellow, and purple)
* 1 small notebook or pad of paper
* 1 pen or pencil
* 1 small envelope or sealed container

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, write the name of one of the colors (e.g., "blue") on a piece of paper, fold it, and place it in the envelope or sealed container. This will be your prediction.
2. **Introduction:** Show the spectator the 5 colored cards and explain that you will try to predict the color they will choose.
3. **Color Selection:** Shuffle the colored cards and ask the spectator to choose one at random. They should not show you the card they have chosen.
