**Trick Name: "Mystery Bloom"**

**Effect:** A small, artificial flower appears to bloom in a spectator's hand, leaving them with a beautiful, fragrant souvenir.

**Materials:**

* A small, artificial flower (e.g., a silk rose or orchid) with a hidden compartment or a small pouch attached to the stem
* A small packet of potpourri or scented powder
* A handkerchief or small piece of cloth
* A tiny, hidden container or capsule (e.g., a small plastic vial or a metal charm) filled with a few grains of the scented powder
* A small, decorative box or container with a lid (optional)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, fill the hidden container or capsule with a few grains of the scented powder. Attach the container to the stem of the artificial flower, making sure it's secure and hidden from view. Place the flower in the decorative box or container, if using.
2. **Introduction:** Begin by introducing the artificial flower and explaining that it's a special, magical bloom that will appear to come to life in the spectator's hand.
3. **Handkerchief Placement:** Ask the spectator
