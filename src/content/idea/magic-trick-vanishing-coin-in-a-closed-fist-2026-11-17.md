**Magic Trick: "Vanishing Coin in a Closed Fist"**

**Materials:**

* A coin (any denomination, but a larger coin like a quarter or half-dollar works best)
* A handkerchief or small piece of cloth (approximately 6-8 inches square)
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the coin on the back of your hand, and cover it with the handkerchief. Make sure the coin is centered on the handkerchief.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the coin on the back of your hand, covered with the handkerchief. Explain that you will make the coin disappear.
3. **Misdirection:** Ask a spectator to hold out their hand, palm up. This will distract the audience and create a sense of anticipation.
4. **Sleight of Hand:** Slowly lift the handkerchief, keeping your hand flat and your fingers together. As you lift the handkerchief, use your thumb to secretly push the coin off the back of your hand and onto your palm. Keep your palm facing downwards to conceal the coin.
