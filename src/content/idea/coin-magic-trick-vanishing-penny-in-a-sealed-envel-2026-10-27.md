**Coin Magic Trick: "Vanishing Penny in a Sealed Envelope"**

**Materials:**

* 1 penny
* 1 small envelope (e.g., a stamp envelope)
* 1 table or other flat surface
* 1 handkerchief or small piece of cloth
* 1 pen or pencil

**Preparation:**

1. Before performing the trick, secretly place a duplicate penny in the envelope and seal it. This will be the "decoy" penny.
2. Make sure the envelope is sealed and the flap is closed.

**Performance:**

**Step 1: Introduction and Setup (Misdirection)**

1. Show the audience the penny and the sealed envelope.
2. Explain that you will make the penny disappear and reappear in the sealed envelope.
3. Ask a spectator to hold the envelope and verify that it is sealed.
4. While the spectator is holding the envelope, use the handkerchief to wipe your hands, creating a distraction.

**Step 2: Coin Placement (Sleight of Hand)**

1. Take the penny from the table and place it on the back of your hand, between your thumb and index finger.
2. Close your fingers over the penny, making a fist.
