**Magic Trick: "Vanishing Coin in the Box"**

**Materials:**

* A small, decorative box (e.g., a jewelry box or a small wooden box) with a lid
* A coin (any denomination)
* A handkerchief or a small piece of cloth
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation**: Place the coin on the back of your hand, and cover it with the handkerchief. Make sure the coin is in the center of the handkerchief.
2. **Introduction**: Show the audience the coin and the box, and explain that you will make the coin disappear into the box.
3. **Misdirection**: Hold the box in one hand and the handkerchief-covered coin in the other. Use your body language to draw attention to the box, saying something like, "I'll just open the box and show you it's empty."
