**Trick Name: "Vanishing Coin in the Box"**

**Materials:**

* A small, empty matchbox or a similar-sized box
* A coin (any denomination will do, but a quarter or a half-dollar coin works well)
* A handkerchief or a small piece of cloth
* A table or other flat surface to perform on

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the coin on the back of your hand, and cover it with the handkerchief. Make sure the coin is centered and the handkerchief is evenly spread out.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the matchbox and the handkerchief-covered coin. Explain that you'll be making the coin disappear from under the handkerchief and reappear inside the matchbox.
3. **Misdirection:** Hold the handkerchief-covered coin in one hand, and use the other hand to open the matchbox. As you open the box, use a bit of misdirection by saying something like, "Now, I'll just open the box to show you it's empty." This will distract the audience from the coin.
