Here's a unique magic trick idea using everyday household objects that's safe, easy to learn, and suitable for beginners:

**Trick Name: "The Vanishing Peanut"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small cups or containers (e.g., plastic cups, paper cups, or even empty yogurt containers)
* 1 small peanut or a similar small object (e.g., a bead, a button, or a small toy)
* 1 small piece of paper or a paper towel
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation**: Place the peanut under one of the cups, let's say Cup A. Make sure to remember which cup the peanut is under.
2. **Introduction**: Show the audience the 3 cups and the peanut. Explain that you will make the peanut disappear and reappear under one of the cups.
3. **Initial Shuffle**: Place the cups upside down on the table, with the cup containing the peanut (Cup A) in the middle. Use the paper or paper towel to cover the cups, making sure to keep the cups in the same position.
4. **The Shuffle**: Slowly lift the paper or paper towel, and use it to shuffle the cups around
