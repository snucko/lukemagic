**Magic Trick: "The Vanishing Peanut"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small cups or containers (e.g., plastic cups, tea cups, or small bowls)
* 1 small peanut or a similar small object (e.g., a bead, a small stone, or a coffee bean)
* A small piece of paper or a paper towel
* A table or other flat surface

**Objective:**
To make the peanut disappear from under one of the cups, leaving the audience wondering where it went.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the peanut on the table in front of you. Cover it with the piece of paper or paper towel.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the peanut under the paper and explain that you will make it disappear.
3. **Cup Placement:** Place the three cups upside down on the table, in a row, about 6-8 inches apart.
4. **Peanut Placement:** Lift the paper and secretly drop the peanut under one of the cups (e.g., the middle cup). Make sure to keep your hands and arms close to your body to avoid revealing the peanut's location.
5. **Mixing the Cups:** Slowly lift and move each cup
