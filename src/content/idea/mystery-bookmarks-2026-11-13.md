**Trick Name: "Mystery Bookmarks"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* 4 small bookmarks or paper strips (each with a different color or design)
* A small piece of paper and a pen for prediction

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Shuffle the deck and deal 4 cards face down onto a table or surface. These cards will be the "destination" cards.
2. **Bookmark Placement:** Place a bookmark on top of each of the 4 face-down cards. Make sure the bookmarks are clearly visible and distinct from one another.
3. **Prediction:** Ask a spectator to choose one of the bookmarks (e.g., "Which color do you like best?"). Once they've chosen a bookmark, use the pen and paper to write down the name of the bookmark they chose (e.g., "Red Bookmark").
