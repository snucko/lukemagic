Here's a unique, original, and self-working card trick that's perfect for beginners:

**Trick Name: "Mystery Mirror"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* A small piece of paper and a pen
* A table or other flat surface

**Preparation:**

1. Shuffle the deck of cards thoroughly.
2. Ask a spectator to choose a card from the deck, but not to show it to you. This card will be their "secret" card.
3. Have the spectator sign their name across the face of their secret card. This will help them verify that it's their card later.
4. Take the deck back from the spectator and place it on the table in front of you.

**Performance:**

1. Explain to the spectator that you will try to find their secret card using a magical "mirror" that will reveal the card's identity.
2. Place the piece of paper on the table next to the deck of cards.
