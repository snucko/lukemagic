**Trick Name: "Mystery Mirror Match"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* A small mirror (approximately 2-3 inches in diameter)
* A table or other flat surface
* A pen or pencil

**Objective:**
To create a magic trick where a spectator's selected card is revealed to match a card that has been "reflected" in a small mirror, creating an illusion of prediction and synchronicity.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Shuffle the deck of cards and place it on the table in front of you.
