**Trick Name: "Mystic Knots"**

**Materials:**

* A piece of rope or string (approx. 3-4 feet long)
* A small wooden or plastic ring (like a keyring or a small hoop)
* A handkerchief or a small piece of cloth
* A table or a flat surface to perform on
