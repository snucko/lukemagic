**Magic Trick: "The Vanishing Treasure"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small, identical cups (plastic or paper)
* 1 small, shiny object (e.g., a metal washer, a small stone, or a bead)
* 1 small piece of cloth or a handkerchief
* A flat surface, such as a table or countertop

**Objective:**
