**Trick Name: "Mystic Knots"**

**Materials:**

* A 3-foot long piece of colored rope or string (preferably with a smooth texture)
* A small, decorative ring or pendant (optional)
* A table or flat surface to perform on

**Effect:**
The magician shows a single piece of rope with a knot in the middle. They then ask a spectator to hold one end of the rope, while the magician holds the other end. With a magical gesture, the knot suddenly disappears, and the rope becomes a single, unknotted length. As a bonus, the decorative ring or pendant appears attached to the rope, as if it was magically tied on.
