**Magic Trick: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Effect:** The magician predicts a color chosen by a spectator from a set of colored cards, and the prediction is revealed in a surprising way.

**Materials:**

* A set of 5 colored cards (red, blue, green, yellow, and purple)
* A small, sealed envelope with a prediction written on a piece of paper inside
* A random number generator (optional, can be replaced with a random number selection method)
* A small, decorative box or container with a hidden compartment (optional)
