Here's a unique and original rope magic trick idea that's perfect for beginners:

**Trick Name: "Mystic Knots"**

**Materials:**

* A piece of white or colored rope (about 3-4 feet long)
* A small piece of paper or a business card
* A pen or pencil
* A table or flat surface

**Effect:**
The magician ties a knot in the rope and then, with a wave of their hand, the knot disappears. But that's not all - the magician then reveals that the knot has reappeared on a piece of paper, drawn in the exact same pattern as the original knot.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before performing the trick, tie a small knot in the rope and then untie it. This will create a small crease in the rope where the knot was.
2. **Introduction:** Show the rope to the audience and explain that you will be tying a special knot that will disappear.
3. **Tying the Knot:** Tie a knot in the rope, making sure to tie it in the same spot where the crease is. Hold the rope up to show the audience the knot.
