**Trick Name: "Mystic Link"**

**Materials:**

* 2 pieces of colored string or rope (approximately 12-18 inches long each)
* 1 small ring or keychain
* 1 table or flat surface
* 1 handkerchief or small piece of cloth

**Objective:**
To make the audience believe that the two separate strings are magically linked together, with the ring or keychain appearing on the linked strings.
