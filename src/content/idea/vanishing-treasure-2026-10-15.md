Here's a unique magic trick idea that's perfect for beginners, using everyday household objects:

**Trick Name: "Vanishing Treasure"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small, identical cups (e.g., plastic or paper cups)
* 1 small, shiny object (e.g., a metal washer, a small stone, or a bead)
* 1 small piece of paper or a sticky note
* 1 pen or pencil
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the small, shiny object under one of the cups, but don't let the audience see which one. You can do this by secretly dropping the object under a cup while setting up the trick.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the 3 cups and the small, shiny object. Explain that you'll be playing a game of "Treasure Hunt" and that the object will disappear and reappear under one of the cups.
