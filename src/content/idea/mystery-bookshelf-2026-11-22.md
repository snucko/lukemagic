**Trick Name: "Mystery Bookshelf"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* A small notebook or a piece of paper with a grid drawn on it (5x5 or 6x6)
* A pen or pencil

**Effect:**
The magician asks a spectator to choose a book (represented by a card) from a "bookshelf" (the grid on the paper). The spectator then finds their chosen book on the "bookshelf" and notes its position. Without any manipulation, the magician reveals the exact location of the chosen book on the "bookshelf".

1. **Preparation:** Before performing the trick, create a grid on the paper with 5 rows and 5 columns (or 6x6, depending on your preference). Number each cell in the grid from 1 to 25 (or 36).
