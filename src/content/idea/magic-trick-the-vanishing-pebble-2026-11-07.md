**Magic Trick: "The Vanishing Pebble"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small, identical cups (plastic or paper)
* 1 small pebble or stone (about 1-2 cm in diameter)
* 1 small piece of cloth or handkerchief
* A flat surface, such as a table or countertop
