**Trick Name: "Dream Destination"**

**Effect:** The magician appears to read the spectator's mind and predict their dream vacation destination.

**Materials:**

* A small, sealed envelope with a prediction written on a piece of paper inside
* A map of the world (can be printed or displayed on a tablet/computer)
* A small notebook and pen
* A random selection of 5-6 destination cards (e.g., Paris, Tokyo, New York, Sydney, etc.) with a unique code on each card (e.g., a letter or number)

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, write a prediction on a piece of paper (e.g., "You will choose Tokyo as your dream destination") and seal it in the envelope. Make sure to keep the envelope in a safe place where it won't be tampered with.
