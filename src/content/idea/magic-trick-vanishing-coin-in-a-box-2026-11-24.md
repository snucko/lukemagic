**Magic Trick: "Vanishing Coin in a Box"**

**Materials:**

* A small, decorative box (e.g., a wooden or plastic box, approximately 2 inches x 1 inch x 1 inch)
* A coin (any denomination, but a quarter or half-dollar works well)
* A handkerchief or small piece of cloth
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the coin on the back of your hand, and cover it with the handkerchief. Make sure the coin is centered and the handkerchief is evenly spread over your hand.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the coin and the box, and explain that you will make the coin disappear from the box.
3. **Misdirection:** Hold the box in one hand and the handkerchief-covered coin in the other. Use your body language to draw attention to the box, saying something like, "I'll place the coin in this box, and then... magic!"
4. **Sleight of Hand:** As you say "magic," use your fingers to secretly drop the coin from the back of your hand onto your lap or a nearby surface, keeping it hidden under the handker
