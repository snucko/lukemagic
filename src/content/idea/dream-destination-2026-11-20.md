**Trick Name: "Dream Destination"**

**Effect:** The performer appears to read the spectator's mind and predicts the destination they've been dreaming of visiting.

**Materials:**

* A small, sealed envelope with a prediction written on a piece of paper inside
* A map of the world (printed or digital)
* A small notebook and pen
* A random selection of 5-6 destination cards (e.g., Paris, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, etc.)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, write down a prediction on a piece of paper, such as "You've always wanted to visit Paris." Seal the paper in the envelope and keep it in a safe place.
2. **Introduction:** Begin by introducing the trick and explaining that you'll be attempting to read the spectator's mind.
3. **Destination Selection:** Show the spectator the map of the world and ask them to think of a place they've always wanted to visit. Emphasize that they shouldn't tell you the destination, but rather keep it a secret.
