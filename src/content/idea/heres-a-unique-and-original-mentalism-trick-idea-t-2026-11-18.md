**Trick Name: "Time Traveler's Prediction"**

**Effect:** The performer appears to predict the exact time a spectator will choose on an imaginary clock, creating an uncanny impression of mind-reading and precognition.

**Materials:**

1. A small, blank piece of paper (around 2x2 inches)
2. A pen
3. A watch or a phone with a clock app
4. A small envelope or a sealed container (optional)
