**Magic Trick: "Vanishing Coin in a Sealed Envelope"**

**Materials:**

* 1 coin (quarter or similar size)
* 1 small envelope (approximately 3 x 4 inches)
* 1 pen or pencil
* 1 small piece of paper (for a "prediction" note)
* 1 table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before performing the trick, write a prediction note on the small piece of paper, such as "The coin has vanished!" Fold the note in half and place it on the table.
2. **Introduction:** Show the coin to the audience and explain that you will make it disappear. Place the coin on the back of your hand, and with your other hand, pick up the envelope and pen.
3. **Misdirection:** Ask a spectator to hold the envelope and sign their name across the seal. This creates a distraction and makes the audience focus on the spectator's action.
4. **Coin Drop:** While the spectator is signing the envelope, secretly drop the coin from the back of your hand into your lap or onto the table, using a gentle tossing motion. This is a basic sleight of hand technique.
