**Trick Name: "Mystical Mirror Match"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards (without jokers)
* A small piece of paper or a sticky note
* A pen or pencil
* A table or flat surface

**Preparation:**

1. Shuffle the deck of cards thoroughly to ensure they are in a random order.
2. Take the top 10 cards from the deck and place them face down on the table in a row.
3. On the piece of paper or sticky note, write a prediction, such as "The 5th card will match the 7th card." You can choose any two positions, but for this example, we'll use the 5th and 7th cards.

**Performance:**

1. Show the prediction to the audience and explain that you will try to find a matching pair of cards in the row.
2. Ask a spectator to choose a card from the remaining deck (not from the row of 10 cards). This card will be used as a "key" card.
3. Take the key card and place it face down on top of the 10-card row, but not in the row itself. This card will serve as a reference point.
