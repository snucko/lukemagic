**Magic Trick: "Vanishing Coin in a Flash"**

**Materials:**

* 1 coin (any denomination, but a larger coin like a quarter or half-dollar works best)
* 1 small piece of paper (about 2x2 inches)
* 1 table or other flat surface
* 1 small flashlight or penlight

**Objective:**
To make the coin disappear from the piece of paper, using misdirection and sleight of hand.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the coin on the table in front of you. Show the coin to the audience and make sure they see it clearly.
2. **Introduction:** Introduce the piece of paper and explain that you will use it to cover the coin. Place the paper over the coin, making sure to cover it completely.
3. **Misdirection:** Turn on the flashlight and shine it on the paper, saying "Now, I'll use this light to make the coin disappear!" Move the light around the paper in a circular motion, creating a distraction.
4. **Sleight of Hand:** While moving the light, use your fingers to secretly palm the coin. To do this, place your fingers under the paper and gently lift the coin
