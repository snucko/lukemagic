**Magic Trick: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Effect:** The magician predicts a randomly selected color from a set of colored cards, which is then revealed to match a previously sealed prediction.

**Materials:**

* A set of 5-6 colored cards (e.g., red, blue, green, yellow, orange, and purple)
* A small envelope or sealed container
* A piece of paper and a pen
* A random number generator (optional)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the trick, write a prediction on a piece of paper, such as "The chosen color is blue." Fold the paper and seal it in the envelope or container.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the set of colored cards and explain that you will predict the color they will choose.
3. **Color Selection:** Ask a spectator to randomly select a colored card from the set. You can use a random number generator to generate a number between 1 and 6, and have the spectator choose the corresponding colored card.
4. **Color Reveal:** Have the spectator reveal their chosen color to the audience.
5. **Prediction Reveal:** Open the sealed envelope or container and read out the prediction. Show the audience
