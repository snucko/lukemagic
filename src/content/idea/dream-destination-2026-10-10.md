Here's a unique and original mentalism trick idea that's perfect for beginners:

**Trick Name: "Dream Destination"**

**Effect:** The performer appears to read the spectator's mind and predict their dream vacation destination.

**Materials:**

* A small, blank map of the world (printed or laminated)
* A set of 5-6 small, colored stickers or dots (different colors)
* A pen or pencil
* A small piece of paper and an envelope (for the prediction)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Before the performance, secretly write the names of 5-6 popular tourist destinations (e.g., Paris, Tokyo, New York, Sydney, Rio, etc.) on small pieces of paper, fold them, and place them in a bowl or hat. Make sure to include a mix of cities and countries.
2. **Introduction:** Introduce yourself as a mentalist and explain that you'll be attempting to read the spectator's mind to predict their dream vacation destination.
3. **Map Presentation:** Show the spectator the blank map of the world and explain that you'll be using it to help them visualize their dream destination.
4. **Sticker Selection:** Ask the spectator to choose a colored sticker
