Here's a unique and original magic trick idea that uses everyday household objects, perfect for beginners:

**Trick Name: "Vanishing Pebble"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small, identical cups (e.g., plastic or paper cups)
* 1 small pebble or marble
* 1 small piece of paper or a coaster
* A table or other flat surface

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the pebble under one of the cups, let's say Cup A. Make sure to remember which cup the pebble is under.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the three cups and the pebble. Explain that you will make the pebble disappear and reappear under one of the cups.
3. **Shuffle the Cups:** Place the cups on the table, with the cup containing the pebble (Cup A) in the middle. Quickly shuffle the cups around, moving them from one position to another. This will create the illusion that the pebble could be under any of the cups.
