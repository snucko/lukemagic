Here's a unique card magic trick for beginners that's easy to learn and doesn't involve making things disappear:

**Trick Name: "Mystery Match"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* A small piece of paper and a pen
* A table or other flat surface to perform on

**Objective:**
To predict the exact match of two cards chosen by a spectator, without them revealing the cards to you.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Shuffle the deck of cards and place it on the table in front of you.
2. **Spectator Selection:** Ask a spectator to choose two cards from the deck, but not to show them to you. They should keep the cards face down in their hands.
3. **Prediction:** Take the piece of paper and pen, and write down a prediction of the two cards you think the spectator has chosen. For example, you might write "5 of Hearts and 8 of Diamonds". Fold the paper in half to conceal your prediction.
4. **Reveal the First Card:** Ask the spectator to reveal one of the cards they chose. Let's say they reveal the 5 of Hearts.
