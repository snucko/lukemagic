**Trick Name: "Mystic Color Match"**

**Materials:**

* 5 small colored stones or beads (red, blue, green, yellow, and purple)
* A small piece of paper and a pen
* A sealed envelope (you can use a plain white envelope)
* A prediction box or a small container with a lid (optional)

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation**: Before the performance, secretly write down a prediction on the piece of paper, such as "The chosen color will be... BLUE". Fold the paper and place it in the sealed envelope. If you have a prediction box, you can place the envelope inside the box and close the lid.
2. **Introduction**: Show the 5 colored stones or beads to the audience and explain that you will ask a spectator to choose one of the colors.
3. **Spectator's Choice**: Ask a spectator to choose a color from the 5 options. To make it more engaging, you can ask them to close their eyes, point to one of the colors, and say "I choose this color".
