**Trick Name: "Mystery Bookshelf"**

**Materials:**

* A standard deck of 52 playing cards
* A small piece of paper and a pen
* A book or a small box with a lid (to act as a "bookshelf")

**Setup:**

1. Shuffle the deck of cards and ask a spectator to choose a card at random. Let's call this card the "target card."
2. Take the top card from the deck and place it on the "bookshelf" (the book or small box). This card will be the "key card."
3. Ask the spectator to sign their name across the face of their target card. This is to ensure that the card is clearly identifiable as theirs.
4. Place the signed target card on top of the deck.

1. Introduce the "bookshelf" and explain that it's a special repository for important books (or cards).
