**Trick Name: "Vanishing Treasure Chest"**

**Materials:**

* A small, decorative treasure chest (or a small wooden box) with a lid that can be opened and closed
* 2-3 small coins (quarters or dollar coins work well)
* A handkerchief or small piece of cloth
* A table or other flat surface to perform on

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation**: Place the treasure chest on the table, with the lid closed. Secretly palm one of the coins in your dominant hand.
2. **Introduction**: Show the audience the treasure chest and explain that it's said to contain a valuable treasure. Open the lid to reveal that it's empty.
3. **Coin Placement**: Place one of the remaining coins on top of the treasure chest, making sure to draw attention to it. Use your non-dominant hand to place the coin, keeping your dominant hand (with the palmed coin) out of sight.
4. **Misdirection**: Use your non-dominant hand to cover the coin on top of the treasure chest with the handkerchief. As you do this, use your dominant hand to secretly drop the palmed coin into your pocket or onto your lap.
