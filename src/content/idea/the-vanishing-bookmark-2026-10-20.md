**Trick Name: "The Vanishing Bookmark"**

**Materials:**

* A bookmark with a distinctive design or color
* A small piece of paper with a prediction written on it (e.g., "The bookmark will disappear on page 7")
* A book with at least 7 pages (any book will do)
* A small envelope or pouch
* A table or other flat surface to perform on

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation**: Before the trick, secretly write the prediction on the small piece of paper and fold it up. Place the folded paper in the envelope or pouch and seal it.
2. **Introduction**: Show the bookmark to the audience and explain that you'll be using it to mark a special page in the book.
3. **Setup**: Open the book to a random page (let's say page 3) and place the bookmark on that page. Close the book and show the audience that the bookmark is still in place.
4. **Prediction**: Show the audience the sealed envelope or pouch and explain that it contains a prediction about what will happen to the bookmark.
