**Magic Trick: "The Mysterious Time Traveler's Box"**

**Props:**

* A small, decorative wooden box (approximately 6 inches long, 4 inches wide, and 2 inches tall) with a lid that can be opened and closed
* A small, battery-operated clock (digital or analog) that can be set to display a specific time
* A piece of paper and a pen
* A small, sealed envelope with a prediction written on a piece of paper inside
* A table or pedestal to display the box and clock

**Setup:**

1. Before the performance, set the clock to a specific time, for example, 3:45 PM.
2. Write a prediction on a piece of paper, such as "The time will be 3:45 PM," and seal it in the envelope.
3. Place the envelope on the table or pedestal, next to the box and clock.

**Performance:**

1. Introduce the trick by explaining that you have a mysterious box that can travel through time.
2. Show the audience the box and clock, and ask them to verify the current time displayed on the clock.
