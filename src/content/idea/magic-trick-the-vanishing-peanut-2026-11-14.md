**Magic Trick: "The Vanishing Peanut"**

**Materials:**

* 3 small cups or containers (e.g., plastic cups, tea cups, or small bowls)
* 1 small peanut or a similar-sized object (e.g., a bead, a small stone, or a coffee bean)
* 1 small piece of paper or a coaster
* A table or other flat surface

**Objective:**
To make the peanut disappear from under one of the cups, leaving the audience wondering where it went.

**Step-by-Step Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** Place the peanut on the piece of paper or coaster on the table. This will be the starting position.
2. **Introduction:** Show the audience the peanut on the paper and introduce the three cups. Explain that you will place the cups over the peanut and mix them up, and then they have to guess which cup the peanut is under.
3. **Cup Placement:** Place one cup over the peanut, making sure it's centered and the peanut is hidden from view.
4. **Mixing the Cups:** Place the other two cups on either side of the cup with the peanut, about 6-8 inches apart. Slowly and deliberately mix the cups by sliding them around
